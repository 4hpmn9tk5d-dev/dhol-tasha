ढोल पाठशाळा

Learn the traditional Maharashtrian dhol — one haat at a time. Thapi in the left hand, thoka in the right.

90 bpm
पहिला हात · The First Haat
हात निवडा · Choose your Haat

Write bols separated by spaces: ta, dhin, pause. Join bols with no space for quick strokes — dhindhin, dhinta, tadhindhin. Write ta^ to raise the left hand high before that ta.

थापी · Thapi

The open left palm strikes the bass side of the dhol, producing the warm, rounded Ta. Keep the wrist loose and let the palm bounce off the skin — the sound should bloom, not slap flat.

Left hand · Bol: Ta

ठोका · Thoka

The curved cane stick in the right hand cracks against the treble side for the sharp, ringing Dhin. The power comes from the wrist's snap, not the arm — strike and release in one motion.

Right hand · Bol: Dhin
